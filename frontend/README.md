# AM Management Group Sdn. Bhd.

> Corporate Website & CMS — Product, Content, UI/UX & Development Specification

## Overview

AM Management Group Sdn. Bhd. is a diversified Malaysian business group with business activities across multiple sectors.

The corporate website is designed around the concept:

**One Group. Multiple Businesses. Diverse Expertise.**

The website will present the main group company and its sister concerns/business divisions in a professional, modern and scalable corporate platform.

> **Primary Market:** Malaysia  
> **Website Type:** Corporate Group / Multi-Company Business Website  
> **Project Status:** Initial Product & Development Specification

## Business Group Structure

### Main Company

**AM Management Group Sdn. Bhd.**

Primary positioning:

- Group Management
- Investment
- Property / Asset-related activities
- Plantation-related investment
- Business development
- Group-level operations

### Sister Concerns

| Company | Category | Business Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **BM Magnitude Services** | Cleaning / Services | Cleaning, commercial cleaning, facility/site services, operational workforce |
| **Hidensypro Sdn. Bhd.** | Machinery & Engineering | Machinery, engineering, industrial equipment, technical services |
| **CM Plantation Services** | Plantation & Agriculture | Plantation operations, agricultural services, field operations, plantation management |
| **AM Multi Trade Empire** | Retail & Trading | Mini market, retail products, consumer goods, daily essentials |
| **MA Travel and Tour Sdn. Bhd.** | Travel & Tourism | Ticketing, travel services, tour services, booking and travel coordination |

> **Content verification:** Some proposed service categories require final client approval before publication. Official company information, addresses, contact details and business classifications should be verified with the client.

## Business Sectors

The website groups the business portfolio into six major sectors:

1. **Management & Investment**
2. **Cleaning & Facility Services**
3. **Engineering & Machinery**
4. **Plantation & Agriculture**
5. **Retail & Trading**
6. **Travel & Tourism**

## Website Goals

The primary objectives are to:

- Create a scalable digital platform for future business expansion

# UI/UX & Design Direction

## Design Style

**Modern Corporate + Premium + Clean + Trustworthy**

### Avoid

- Excessive gradients
- Too many animations
- Cheap stock imagery
- Overcrowded cards
- Excessive colors
- Too much text
- Generic template feeling

### Use

- Large typography
- Strong whitespace
- Premium photography
- Subtle animations
- Clean cards
- Professional icons
- Structured grid
- Strong CTA

## Responsive Design

The website must support:

| Device | Target Width |
|---|---:|
| Desktop | 1920px |
| Desktop | 1440px |
| Desktop | 1280px |
| Laptop | 1024px |
| Tablet | 768px |
| Mobile | 425px |
| Mobile | 390px |
| Mobile | 375px |

**Mobile-first development is recommended.**
